Pretty designs but the paper is so thin that my markers bleed through to the next page. I ended up only using every other page.

The flowers are lovely, however the designs are far too detailed for my mother who has low vision. She gave up after two pages.

I wish it had single sided pages. The pictures are printed on both sides so you cannot use anything wetter than a colored pencil.

Great variety, no two pages are the same, and the thick lines make it easy to stay inside. Very relaxing after work.

Several of the pictures look like the same picture flipped around. Repetitive. Also a couple of pages were blurry like a low resolution scan.

Bought this as a gift for my grandma and she loves it. Big simple shapes, nothing fussy. Would be nice if the book were a little bigger.
